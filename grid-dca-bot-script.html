What Makes This GRID + DCA Bot Different?

The bot is designed to combine regular grid trading with a dynamic DCA process. It can start from the current market price rather than requiring a fixed starting price range.

Start at Current Price

Start the grid from the market price when you activate the bot. The initial buy order is 1 USDT.

Regular GRID Sell

A regular grid buy is assigned a sell target 4% above its buy price, subject to execution and market conditions.

Dynamic DCA

When the market drops, the next DCA level and lot size are determined using the cumulative price drop after the previous buy.

Wait-for-Recovery Buy

The bot waits while the Ask Price is not above the previous day's high. When the Ask Price rises above that high, it can place the next DCA buy.

Single or Global Sell

DCA positions can be sold at the configured target: 4% above the last buy price, or the 3% global target for the complete grid, according to the strategy rules.

0.2% Lot-Size Compounding

After every successful sell—regular single sell, DCA sell or global grid sell—the next trading lot size increases by 0.2% over the previous lot size.

How the Trading Strategy Works

A simplified overview of the bot's operating logic.

Regular GRID Cycle

  1. The bot starts at the current market price.
  2. It places the initial buy using a 1 USDT lot.
  3. A sell target is set 4% above that buy price.
  4. After a successful sell, the next lot size increases by 0.2%.

Dynamic DCA Cycle

  1. The bot tracks the price drop after the previous buy.
  2. The cumulative drop determines the next DCA level and lot size.
  3. It waits until the Ask Price moves above the previous day's high before making the next DCA buy.
  4. DCA and global sell rules are applied as configured.
  5. Every successful sell triggers the 0.2% increase in the next lot size.

Order placement, fills, fees, minimum order sizes and available balance can affect actual execution and results.
