Bot Trading Strategy

Sakhiraj Grid Bot follows an advanced Grid + DCA + Compounding + Breakout Entry strategy for KuCoin Spot Trading. The bot works on predefined rules and automatically manages buy and sell cycles without indicators or manual decision making.

1. Initial Buy

When the bot starts a new trading cycle, it places the first Market Buy.

  • Uses 0.5% of available trading capital.
  • Minimum order size is 1 USDT.
  • The higher value between these two is used.

2. Breakout Based Entry

The bot does not continuously buy in a falling market. A new trading cycle starts only after market strength confirmation.

  • Current Ask price must break above the previous day's high.
  • After breakout confirmation, the bot starts managing Grid Buy levels.
  • This avoids unnecessary early entries during weak market conditions.

3. Grid Buying with Compounding

After the first buy, the bot places additional buys when the price moves downward.

  • Minimum Grid distance is 1.5% price drop from the previous buy.
  • Every additional drop activates the next Grid level.
  • Buy amount increases automatically using a small compounding multiplier.

4. Dynamic Capital Management

The bot automatically adjusts capital usage according to market movement.

  • Small drops use smaller additional funds.
  • Deeper drops activate more Grid levels.
  • The more the price falls, the more capital is gradually deployed.
  • Compounding helps increase the order size step by step.

5. Individual Sell

Each buy position is managed according to the regular Take Profit target.

  • Individual sell target is approximately 3% profit.
  • Completed positions are removed from the active cycle.
  • Remaining positions continue until their sell condition is reached.

6. Global Exit

The bot continuously monitors the combined performance of all active positions.

When the combined portfolio reaches the Global Target, all remaining active buy orders are sold together.

  • Releases trading capital.
  • Completes the current trading cycle.
  • Prepares the bot for the next opportunity.
